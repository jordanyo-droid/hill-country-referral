What we are

Hill Country Referral Services is a referral service. We are not a contractor. We do not perform, supervise or guarantee any repair or other work. Independent companies do the work, and they are responsible for their own work, pricing, permits and warranties.

Licensed and insured companies

We refer homeowners only to companies that carry insurance and, in trades where Texas requires a license, hold a current license. We confirm this before sending requests and keep proof on file. You are still free to check any company yourself, for example through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation at tdlr.texas.gov.

Cost to you

There is no charge to homeowners for our matching service. The company gives you its own quote, and you decide whether to hire it.

Service call fee

When you approve a repair, we cover the company's standard service call or diagnostic fee, as confirmed with you when you book. The company will tell you its fee before it visits. Details may vary by company and job and are confirmed before the visit.

Emergencies

If you smell gas, see smoke or fire, or someone is in danger, leave the home and call 911. Do not wait for us.
