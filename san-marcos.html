Common questions

Are you a contractor?

No. We're a referral service. Independent companies do the work, and they are responsible for their own work, pricing and warranties.

Are the companies licensed and insured?

Yes. We only work with companies that carry insurance, and in every trade where Texas requires a license, they hold a current one. We keep proof on file. You can look up a contractor's license yourself at tdlr.texas.gov.

Is someone available 24/7?

Yes. Text or call any time. We'll confirm what you need and match you with a company that can help.

How does the service call fee work?

Companies usually charge a fee to come out and diagnose the problem. If you approve the repair, we cover that fee, so it doesn't add to your bill. Before the visit, the company will tell you the fee and confirm whether it's covered for your job.

Does it cost me anything to get matched?

No. There's no charge to homeowners for the match. The company gives you its own quote, and you decide whether to hire it.

Will my request go to several companies?

No. We send your request to one company at a time, so you won't get a flood of calls.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

We can't quote prices because we don't do the work. The company will look at the job and quote you directly.

Are you a licensed and insured contractor looking to find more information?

If so, fill out this form and I will reach out to you.

We confirm every company's license and insurance before sending any requests. There's no obligation to learn more.
