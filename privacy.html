Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Hill Country Referral Services ("we", "us") is a referral service. We connect homeowners with independent, licensed and insured home service companies. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, the address where work is needed, and a description of your problem. You give it to us when you text or call us, or use our site.
  • Contractor information: if you are a contractor, your company name, contact details, trade, license number, cities served and proof of insurance.
  • Basic site information: if Google Analytics is turned on, we count visits and actions such as taps on Call or Text. We do not send your name, phone number, email or address to Google Analytics.
  • How you found us: if you arrive from an ad or a shared link, we note which ad or link it was, so we know what is working. This is not tied to other sites you visit.

How we use it

  • To understand what you need and match you with a company that can help.
  • To contact you about your request, including by phone, text message and email.
  • To confirm and keep records of licenses and insurance for companies we work with.
  • To improve our service and keep business records.

Who we share it with

When we match you with a company, we share your name, phone number, email, address and the description of your problem with that company so it can contact you and quote the work. We also use service providers that help us run our business, such as phone, email, website hosting and analytics providers. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share text-message opt-in information or phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing.

Text messages

If you text us, we will reply to you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts and HELP for help. See our Terms and Text Messaging.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hillcountryreferralservices@gmail.com. Companies we shared your information with keep their own records under their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep information as long as we need it to help with your request and to keep business and legal records.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of sending or storing data is completely secure.

Children

Our service is for adults who own or manage a home. It is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
